iPHONE SHARE SHORTCUT — SAVE RECIPE

After Recipe Box is hosted and connected:

1. Open Recipe Box > More.
2. Copy the Shortcut URL shown there.
3. Open Apple's Shortcuts app and tap +.
4. Name it: Save Recipe
5. Open shortcut details and turn on Show in Share Sheet.
6. Set it to receive URLs.
7. Add a URL action.
8. Paste the Recipe Box shortcut URL.
9. Replace SHARED_URL with the Shortcut Input variable.
10. Add Open URLs.
11. Save.

Usage:
TikTok or Instagram > Share > Save Recipe.
Recipe Box opens and stores the post in the shared Inbox.
Both phones will see it.
